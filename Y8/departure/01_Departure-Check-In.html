Checkpoint 2 • 10 minutes

Classroom code

Good routines protect learning time, equipment and people. Judge the choices, then build the correct start-up sequence.

Ready, rethink or stop?

Think

Decide what the student should do. Ready supports learning. Rethink needs a better first step. Stop breaks a clear safety or behaviour rule.

Start-up sequence

Put in order

Move the steps into the order you should follow at the beginning of an ICT lesson.

Move the steps, then check your sequence.
Give one clear reason. Aim for one or two sentences.

ICT non-negotiables

No food or drink near equipment. Do not touch power or network cables. Keep chairs still and feet on the floor. Use only authorised sites and your own school account. Listen when someone else is speaking.

Land the lesson properly

Save with a clear filename, close your work, log off, stand behind your chair, then push it under when directed. Do not shut down unless the teacher asks.

Help ladder

1. Read the current instruction again.
2. Check the example or help panel.
3. Ask a nearby partner quietly, if allowed.
4. Ask the teacher with a specific question.

Independent does not mean silent struggle. It means trying sensible steps before giving up.

Checkpoint 2 ready?Six decisions correct, sequence correct, and a reason written.

Before you report a fault

Check the obvious safely: Is the monitor on? Is the correct account selected? Is the volume muted? Is the browser responding? Do not unplug, swap or pull cables. Report the workstation number and what you can see.

Checkpoint 4 • 7 minutes

Capture the screen

A useful screenshot shows exactly what proves the task. Choose the right tool, keep private information out, and check the image before uploading it.

Two capture tools

Whole screen
PrtSc

Copies everything currently visible on the screen. Use only when the whole display is needed.

Selected area
Win+Shift+S

Lets you drag around the exact evidence. This is usually clearer and safer.

Choose the better tool for each situation.

Situation 1: You need to show one error message, but your email tab is also open.

Situation 2: Your teacher asks for evidence of the complete desktop layout.

Your evidence

Make

Open the correct Year 8 ICT Google Classroom assignment. Use Win + Shift + S to capture only the assignment title and instructions. Do not include another student’s work or a password.

Paste or upload your screenshotClick here, then press Ctrl + V, or use Choose image.

Evidence check: crop with purpose

Before uploading, ask: Can the teacher see the required title and instructions? Is unrelated information removed? Is the text readable? A bigger screenshot is not automatically a better screenshot.

Checkpoint 4 ready?Both tool choices correct and one clear screenshot uploaded.
