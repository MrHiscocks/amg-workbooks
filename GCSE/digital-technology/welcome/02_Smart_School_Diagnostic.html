Need a nudge?

Avoid three versions of the same security risk.

Think across four categories
  • Security: stolen or altered data
  • Privacy: excessive tracking or misuse
  • Reliability: system failure or inaccurate records
  • People: exclusion, mistakes or unfair decisions
Match protections carefully
A backup helps after data loss, but it does not stop an unauthorised user. Ask whether your safeguard actually reduces that specific risk.

Decision builder

Use this only if you need help organising your judgement.

Five-sentence framework
  1. State your decision.
  2. Explain the strongest benefit.
  3. Introduce the most serious risk.
  4. Explain whether it can be reduced.
  5. Conclude why one side matters more.
