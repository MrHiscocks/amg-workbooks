Classroom workflow
1. Distribute the required HTML workbook through Google Classroom.
2. Students enter their name and display ID before saving.
3. Students complete embedded questions, repairs and build tasks.
4. IDLE Workspace pages provide downloadable .py starters for larger programs.
5. Students submit the saved HTML workbook and the requested .py file.
6. The assessment supports Developing, Secure and Mastery outcome badges.

Technical notes
- Workbooks are self-contained HTML files.
- Student identity fields are excluded from progress calculations.
- The templates open on Overview and begin at 0%.
- Name and display ID are required before Save Workbook downloads a file.
- Embedded editor, console and .py download tools remain available.
- Unit 4 uses the lime accent #A9D94B.
