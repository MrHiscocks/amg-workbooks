Balanced interpretation

CAT evidence does not support describing the 2026 group simply as lower-to-middle ability. The school target profile was polarised, while the ALPS minimum profile was two A, five B, four C and one D. Compared with the 2025 mean CAT of 95.3, the 2026 cohort was almost seven points stronger. APS and A*–C both improved, and the corrected ALPS score rose marginally from 1.05 to 1.06. The ALPS report cautions that different years may use different benchmark data, so the movement should be read as stable positive value added rather than a material improvement.
