Balanced interpretation

The corrected ALPS model gives one A, five B, four C and one D minimum expectations. Five pupils met or exceeded these grades and three exceeded them. The subject delivered 55 grade points against 60.00 expected, giving 0.91 and grade 5. This was weaker than the 2025 score of 1.12 and grade 2, although ALPS cautions that annual reports may use different benchmark datasets. The 2026 score was only 0.01 below the grade 4 threshold, while three A/A* outcomes and practical strengths remained important positives.
