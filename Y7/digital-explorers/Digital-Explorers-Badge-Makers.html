Checkpoint 02 · Think it through

Plan your badge

A badge should celebrate a skill someone can demonstrate. “Be good at computers” is too vague. Tell us exactly what they would need to do.

Example: Folder Finder

To earn it: create a subject folder; save three files with names that explain their contents; find and open the correct file without help.

Why it is useful: “This helps me find my work quickly at the start of a lesson. For example, I could open my science presentation without searching through lots of files.”

3. How can an explorer earn it?

Write three specific requirements. Start with an action such as create, organise, check, explain, use or demonstrate. Each one must be something you could show a teacher.

Checkpoint 03 · Make it yours

Create in Canva

Your build instructions

  1. Open Canva using your usual school account.
  2. Start a blank square design. A 500 × 500 px canvas works well.
  3. Add a circle, shield or another bold shape as your badge base.
  4. Add one main image or symbol that represents your chosen skill.
  5. Add your badge name. Make it large enough to read.
  6. Arrange your elements neatly. Use 2–3 main colours and make sure the text stands out.
  7. Give your Canva design a sensible name, such as Folder Finder Badge – Alex.

Make a badge, not a poster

Keep it compact, bold and easy to recognise. The detailed requirements and explanation belong in this workbook.

Build your own design from shapes, images and text. Do not just change the words on a finished badge template.

Stuck for an image?

Think about a symbol: a magnifying glass for searching, a folder for organisation, a compass for problem-solving or linked hands for teamwork.

If an element is unavailable on your account, choose another. You do not need to buy anything.

Scout’s small-size test: zoom out. Can you still read the name and recognise the symbol? If not, make the important parts larger and remove clutter.

Extra challenge: a higher-level badge

How could someone go beyond the basics? Explain one extra requirement for a gold version of your badge.
